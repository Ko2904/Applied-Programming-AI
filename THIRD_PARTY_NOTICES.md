Review the applicable terms and obtain any required permission before
redistributing them.

- **TVMaze:** Series, schedule, people, and related data come from the
  [TVMaze API](https://www.tvmaze.com/api), which licenses API data under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). ScreenScope
  links to TVMaze and documents the attribution and share-alike requirements.
- **TMDB:** Film and trending data come from the [TMDB API](https://developer.themoviedb.org/).
  TMDB's free API is for non-commercial use; commercial use requires a separate
  license. ScreenScope displays the required attribution notice and uses the
  approved [TMDB logo](https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution) in
  its sidebar, with a link to the attribution requirements. Images remain
  subject to their rights holders and current TMDB terms.
- **Books to Scrape:** Catalogue text and images are fetched from the Books to
  Scrape practice site at runtime; no scraped catalogue dataset is included in
  this repository. This repository does not grant rights to that content.
- **New Tab Dashboard:** `quotes.json` contains original daily notes created
  for this project, not third-party quotations.
- **Dependencies:** Python packages used by the projects remain subject to
  their respective licenses and terms.
